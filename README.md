# Limzo 🦎

**Free anti-spam and moderation bot for Telegram groups, with member stats, levels and weekly reports in the same bot.**

[Limzo](https://limzo.com) keeps Telegram groups clean and active. It stops spam, scams and fake admins before they reach your members, then turns group activity into public stats pages, levels and weekly highlights. Every protection layer is included on the free plan.

## Protection

- 🛡️ **Anti-spam** — scam-message detection, flood and duplicate control, and a link safety scan
- 🚪 **Newcomer captcha** — new members verify before they can post
- 🎭 **Fake-admin guard** — catches accounts impersonating your admins
- ⚠️ **`/warn` warnings** with automatic enforcement, plus raid protection and an optional language lock
- 👻 **Ghost-member cleanup** — remove deleted accounts from the member list

More: [Telegram anti-spam bot](https://limzo.com/telegram-anti-spam-bot/) · [Best Telegram anti-spam bots compared](https://limzo.com/best-telegram-anti-spam-bots/)

## Community

- 📊 **Public stats pages** — a live page at `limzo.com/s/<group>` with leaderboards, activity charts and awards
- 🏅 **Levels, badges & achievements** — XP from helpful replies and reactions earned
- 📬 **Weekly highlights & admin reports** — one recap post for the group, a private report for admins
- 🎮 **Mini-games** — daily quiz, word race and more
- 🌍 **13 languages** — en, ru, es, pt, id, tr, fa, it, ar, hi, vi, am, ms

## For developers

- **MCP server** — read-only public group stats for AI assistants: [`limzo-mcp`](https://github.com/LimzoCom/limzo-mcp) (`npx -y limzo-mcp`) or streamable HTTP at `https://limzo.com/api/public/mcp`
